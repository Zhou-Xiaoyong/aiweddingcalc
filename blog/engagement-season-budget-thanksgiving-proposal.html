Engagement season runs from Thanksgiving to Valentine's Day, and 47% of US couples get engaged inside those four months, according to The Knot's 2025 Real Weddings Study. A realistic engagement season budget for that stretch lands near $9,300, and only half of it is the ring.

The other half is what couples discover in January: a second band, the dinner, the photos, the party someone offers to host, and the insurance nobody mentions at the counter. Each line is small. Together they cost about as much as a decent honeymoon.

The Ring: $4,600, and the Rule That Was Advertising

The average engagement ring cost $4,600 for couples who married in 2025, per The Knot's 2026 Real Weddings Study of more than 10,000 couples. That figure has fallen four years running: $6,000 in 2021, $5,500 in 2023, $5,200 in 2024, $4,600 now. Nothing about diamonds got cheaper. Lab-grown center stones did.

Lab-grown stones now account for 61% of engagement ring purchases, a 239% jump since 2020. They average 2.0 carats at $4,300, while natural stones average 1.6 carats at $7,000. Couples are buying a bigger stone for less money, and the national average is following them down.

Which makes the two months' salary rule obsolete twice over. It was never a rule. De Beers wrote it as ad copy, starting with one month's pay and escalating to two by the 1980s. The actual distribution is lopsided: 64% of buyers spend under $6,000, a third spend under $3,000, and only about 8% land between $10,000 and $14,999. A $4,600 average describes almost nobody in particular.

Set the number from cash instead. If $4,600 is the ceiling, you have three levers: stone origin, carat, and setting. A lab-grown stone in a stock solitaire is where the money goes furthest, and it is also the purchase that ships fastest, sometimes in five days. A custom setting with a hand-set natural stone is a four to six week build in November, which puts delivery uncomfortably close to Christmas Eve.

One purchase gets forgotten because it happens in a different room: the band. Our rings budget calculator prices metal, not stones, and the spread is wide. Tungsten runs $150 a band, titanium $200, yellow gold $600, rose gold $650, white gold $700, platinum $1,200. Two white gold bands cost $1,400. Two platinum bands cost $2,400, rarely part of the conversation in December.

Then schedule the ring on your renter's or homeowner's policy before it leaves the store. Standard policies cap jewelry theft at $1,000 to $2,500, so a $4,600 ring is mostly uninsured by default. A scheduled rider runs 1% to 2% of replacement value, or $46 to $92 a year, and it covers loss and mysterious disappearance, which is the clause that matters when a ring goes into a lake.

The Night Itself Costs More Than the Box

A proposal can cost $230 or $1,450, and the difference is rarely the ring. It is the room, the photographer, and whether you booked a hotel.

  • Dinner for two: $150 to $400 at a standard restaurant. A New Year's Eve prix fixe in a major city runs $250 to $450 per person before the 20% service charge and tax, and most of those menus are non-negotiable.
  • A photographer for one hour: $300 to $800. Proposal photographers book out around Thanksgiving and New Year's Eve, so six weeks of lead time is the practical minimum, not a courtesy.
  • Hotel: $200 to $450. Thanksgiving week and the last week of December add 30% to 40% in destination cities, which is the single largest swing in the whole night.
  • Flowers: $80 to $200 for a bouquet. Roses in the week before Valentine's Day roughly double.

What to skip: a videographer ($800 and up), custom signage, and any setup that needs a venue contact. A phone on a tripod covers the same memory for $0, and it is the version most couples watch again.

The Engagement Party, Priced Per Head

Engagement party costs are quoted per head, and the number swings by a factor of five depending on the room.

A backyard or family-home party with catered trays runs $30 to $45 a guest. A private room at a restaurant with passed appetizers and a hosted bar runs $60 to $75. A venue with staff, rentals, and a bar package starts at $100 and climbs. For 30 guests, that is $900 to $3,000 before invitations, cake, and flowers, which add $300 to $600.

The host pays, and the host is whoever offers. Traditionally that was the bride's parents; in 2026 it is just as often a sibling, a friend, or the couple. Skip the second registry. Guests bring a bottle or a small gift, and a "stock the bar" party is the one format where asking for something specific is normal.

Timing matters more than budget here. Three to eight weeks after the proposal works. December 20 does not, because every guest already has three parties that week and the ones who matter most are the ones who cancel. If the proposal lands at Thanksgiving, a mid-January party costs less, books easier, and gets a better turnout. Use the event budget calculator to price the room before you pick the date.

What a Holiday Proposal Does to Your Wedding Date

Here is the inversion nobody explains: proposing at Thanksgiving is peak, and marrying at Thanksgiving is off-peak.

Engagement season is the busiest four months of the year for jewelers, restaurants, and photographers. The wedding calendar runs on a different clock. Late November, after Thanksgiving week, is one of the cheapest stretches to book a venue, with site fees 20% to 30% below June. December is the opposite, where the two or three Saturdays near Christmas carry a holiday premium that most venues write into the contract as a higher minimum rather than a line item.

Same 100 guests, three dates, one budget model:

Date Type Total, 100 Guests Per Guest Venue Site Fee Expected Acceptance
Second Saturday of June$33,322$333Baseline85% to 88%
Saturday after Thanksgiving$30,480$30522% below baseline74% to 78%
Second Saturday of December$36,100$36118% above baseline70% to 74%

The December line is expensive for a reason that is not the site fee. Holiday Saturdays sit inside a two-week window when out-of-town guests have already spent their travel budget, so acceptance runs 70% to 74% instead of the 85% June delivers. That is 26 to 30 empty seats.

Empty seats do not refund anything. A $12,000 food and beverage minimum still bills at that minimum when 74 people sit down, which moves the food cost from $120 a seated guest to $162. That is the real holiday premium: not the surcharge on the invoice, but the cost per person who shows up.

New Year's Eve is its own category. Venues price it as a holiday event, often at 1.5 times a standard Saturday, and bundle a midnight toast and Champagne that you pay for per person. It photographs beautifully. It also competes with every other party in the city for vendors, staff, and hotel rooms.

The cheap version of the same anniversary is one week earlier than you think. A Thanksgiving proposal plus a 12-month engagement lands on the Saturday after Thanksgiving the following year, which is off-peak and $5,620 cheaper for the same 100 people. What to Buy in the Next Six Weeks

From a Thanksgiving proposal to New Year's, there is a short list worth doing early and a longer list worth ignoring until the wedding date exists.

Buy now: the band order, which takes four to six weeks on a custom build, plus ring insurance and the proposal photographer if the date is still ahead of you. Ring sizing is free at almost any jeweler and takes ten minutes.

Wait: save-the-dates before you have a date, a venue before you have a budget, and a gown before you have a season. The gown is the one item with a hard clock. Production runs 16 to 24 weeks and alterations add 6 to 8 weeks, so a December engagement aiming at a June wedding has roughly 26 weeks to work with and needs to order by early January. Miss that, and you are either paying a 10% to 20% rush fee or buying off the rack. Our 12-month timeline sequences the rest of it.

The other December deadline is contractual rather than physical. Venues, photographers, and DJs reprice at the turn of the year, and a 5.5% reset on $21,810 of contracts comes to about $1,397 by the second week of January. The signing order and the deposit bill are laid out in the wedding vendors to book before January 1.

Watch the late-November sales. Black Friday jewelry promotions are real, but they concentrate on stock settings and preset stones. If you are buying a stock solitaire, waiting three weeks can save 15% to 25%. If you are buying custom, the sale does not apply, and the six-week build is the constraint that matters.

One deadline that catches people: save-the-dates go 6 to 9 months ahead, so a June wedding means sending in September. The save-the-date timing guide covers the holiday-weekend exceptions.

Why the Wedding Budget Starts at Zero After Engagement Season

The $34,200 national average for a US wedding covers the wedding day. It does not include the engagement ring, the bands, the proposal, or the party. Those purchases happen 12 to 15 months earlier, on a different card, often before anyone has opened a spreadsheet.

That timing gap is how a wedding budget quietly loses $9,300 in month one. If your total cash for this whole project is $40,000 and $9,300 goes to the engagement, you are planning a $30,700 wedding. At $333 a guest, that is 92 people, not the 118 the average implies.

Two ways to handle it. Keep a separate engagement account and fund it before the wedding savings, which is honest and keeps the wedding number clean. Or put all of it in one number and accept that the guest list absorbs the difference. What does not work is treating the ring as a gift and the wedding as a budget, because the money comes from the same place.

Monthly math, if you are saving: $9,300 over 12 months is $775 a month, or $388 each if you split it. Over six months, the same total needs $1,550 a month, which is where most holiday proposals end up because the decision arrives with the turkey.

Run the wedding number first, then subtract. The wedding budget calculator will split $30,700 across every category and show you what a 90-guest wedding actually costs, and the savings calculator turns the gap into a monthly deposit. If the guest count is the variable you are willing to move, the guest list calculator prices each seat before you send anything.

Propose on Thanksgiving if that is the plan. Then book the Saturday after Thanksgiving 2027, not the second Saturday of December. Same anniversary weekend, 100 guests, $5,620 back in the budget, and a room that is 85% full instead of 72%.

The venue is the single biggest line on most wedding budgets — and the easiest place to get blindsided by hidden fees. With the average US wedding now costing about $34,200 (The Knot 2026 Real Weddings Study), couples who walk into a tour without a prepared list of questions to ask a wedding venue routinely leave with a number that's 30–45% lower than what they'll actually pay. This guide fixes that. It groups 60+ questions by category, tells you what a good answer sounds like, and gives you a scoring system to compare venues on equal footing.

Why the Questions Matter More Than the Tour

A venue tour is staged theater: the lights are dimmed, the sample table is dressed, and the coordinator is at their most charming. The contract you sign six weeks later is not theater. The questions you ask are your only chance to translate the vibe into hard numbers and non-negotiables. Three rules keep you safe:

  • Get the all-in number first. Never compare venues by their menu price or site fee alone — those are the smallest parts of the bill.
  • Write everything down. Bring a printed checklist (use the one at the bottom of this article) and tick answers as you go.
  • Confirm in writing. After the tour, email a one-paragraph recap and ask the coordinator to confirm. Verbal quotes evaporate; written ones protect your deposit.

Availability & Date Questions

You can't negotiate a date the venue doesn't have. Lock availability before you invest emotion:

  • Is our preferred date (and two backups) available, and for how long will you hold it without a deposit?
  • Do you offer a discount for Friday, Sunday, or off-season dates? By how much?
  • Are there other events on the same day, and do we get exclusive use of the space?
  • What time can vendors arrive to set up, and what is our hard end time?
  • If we reschedule (not cancel), is our deposit transferable to a new date?

Good answer: They hold your date 24–48 hours free, quote a specific off-peak discount (often 15–30%), and put exclusive use in writing. Red flag: "We'll figure out the time later" or a refusal to hold the date at all.

Pricing & Total-Cost Questions

This is where budgets are made or broken. Ask for the number that actually hits your credit card:

  • What is the all-in per-person cost including rental, service charge, tax, and every mandatory fee? (This is the only fair comparison number.)
  • What is the food & beverage minimum for our date, and does it drop on off-peak days?
  • Is the service charge the gratuity, or is gratuity additional — and does the service charge reach the staff?
  • What exactly is included in the rental fee (tables, chairs, linens, china, glassware, dance floor, AV, parking, bridal suite)?
  • Are there fees for cake cutting, corkage, cleanup, or a mandatory coordinator?
  • What is the sales-tax rate applied to the bill?

Service charges of 20–25% that don't reach staff, plus separate gratuity, can add 36–44% on top of the menu price. Model the full stack — subtotal, tax, service charge, tips — with our venue cost calculator so the final invoice is never a surprise.

Capacity, Layout & Logistics

A beautiful room that doesn't fit your guest list — or your floor plan — is a dealbreaker:

  • What is the seated dinner capacity, and the capacity for ceremony + reception in the same space?
  • Is the dance floor big enough for ~40% of guests at once? Where does it sit relative to the band or DJ?
  • Are there built-in restrictions on where the ceremony, dinner, and dancing happen?
  • How many restrooms are there, and are any shared with another event?
  • Is there a load-in area for rentals, and a space to store gifts and personal items?

Catering, Bar & Cake Questions

Food and alcohol are usually the venue's biggest add-ons:

  • Do you require in-house catering, or can we bring an outside caterer — and what is the outside-catering fee?
  • Are we required to use your bar, and can we bring our own alcohol (BYOB)? What is the corkage fee?
  • Is there a cake-cutting fee if we use an outside baker?
  • Can we customize the menu for allergies, kids, or dietary needs, and at what cost?
  • Who staffs the bar — your team or ours — and what are their pouring limits?

Vendor & Decoration Restrictions

Restrictions quietly add cost because they force you to rent or hire specific people:

  • Do you have a preferred-vendor list we must use, or can we hire anyone licensed and insured?
  • Are open flames, candles, confetti, sparklers, or hanging installations allowed?
  • Can we attach decor to walls, trees, or tents — and with what (no nails, no tape)?
  • Is there a noise curfew or a music cutoff time?
  • Do you require event liability insurance, and do you sell it or recommend a provider?

Weather Backup & Contingency Plans

For any outdoor or tented wedding, this section protects your day:

  • What is the indoor backup for an outdoor ceremony, and is it the same capacity?
  • Who decides on the call, and by what time, if we move indoors?
  • Is a tent included, or is it a $2,000–$6,000 add-on we'd need to rent?
  • Are there heaters or fans for temperature extremes, and who pays to run them?

Contract, Payment & Cancellation Questions

The contract is the venue. Read it through the lens of these questions:

  • What is the deposit, when is the balance due, and do you offer a payment plan?
  • What is the cancellation and refund policy, and is any of the deposit non-refundable?
  • What is the overtime rate per hour if we run long?
  • Are prices locked for our date, or can they increase before we pay in full?
  • Who is our day-of contact, and what happens if our coordinator leaves the company?

Accessibility, Parking & Guest Experience

Happy guests are a reflection of the venue:

  • Is the ceremony and reception space wheelchair accessible, including restrooms?
  • How much parking is there, is it free or valet, and is there a shuttle option?
  • Are there nearby hotels for out-of-town guests, and do you have room blocks?
  • Is the venue loud, dark, or hard to find at night — and is signage provided?

How to Score & Compare Venues Side by Side

Asking great questions is only half the job; comparing the answers is what saves money. Use a simple 1–5 score for each venue on five axes: Price fit, Date availability, Inclusions (fewer add-on fees = higher), Flexibility (vendors, BYOB, decor), and Vibe match. Then weight Price fit and Inclusions double, because those drive your actual spend. The vendor comparison tool lets you log multiple quotes on the same scale so the winner isn't the prettiest room — it's the one that fits your budget and your must-haves. The One-Page Printable Checklist

Before every tour, print this and tick as you go. Anything blank gets a follow-up email the same day:

  • ☐ All-in per-person price (rental + service charge + tax + fees) in writing
  • ☐ F&B minimum for our exact date, and off-peak discount
  • ☐ Date hold window and exclusive-use confirmation
  • ☐ Exact inclusions list (what we do NOT pay extra for)
  • ☐ BYOB / outside-catering policy and fees
  • ☐ Cake-cutting and corkage fees
  • ☐ Weather backup plan and tent cost
  • ☐ Restrictions (flames, decor, noise curfew, vendors)
  • ☐ Deposit, payment schedule, cancellation & overtime terms
  • ☐ Accessibility, parking, and hotel blocks
  • ☐ Insurance requirement and who provides it
  • ☐ Day-of contact name and contingency if they leave

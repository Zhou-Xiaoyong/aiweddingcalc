How Much Does a 150-Person Wedding Cost in 2026?

The average cost of a 150-person wedding in 2026 is $33,000 – $42,000, with most couples spending around $37,500 for a semi-formal wedding in a mid-cost area.

But here's the thing: your actual cost could be $20,000 or $80,000+ depending on three big factors:

  • Location: A wedding in New York City costs about 50% more than one in the Midwest
  • Formality level: A casual backyard wedding vs. a black-tie ballroom affair
  • Day of week: Saturday weddings are 20% more expensive than Friday or Sunday

For this guide, we'll use the semi-formal, Saturday wedding in a mid-cost area as our baseline — roughly $37,500 total for 150 guests.

Venue & Catering (The Biggest Expense)

Venue and catering typically make up 40-50% of your total wedding budget. For 150 guests, here's what to expect:

Venue Cost

For a Saturday wedding with 150 guests, venue costs range from $4,000 (barn/garden) to $12,000+ (hotel/ballroom). The average is about $6,500, which typically includes:

  • 5-6 hours of event space
  • Tables, chairs, and basic linens
  • Setup and cleanup
  • Parking or valet (sometimes)

Catering Cost

For 150 guests, catering runs $50 – $145 per person, or $7,500 – $21,750 total. The average semi-formal plated dinner is about $95/person, plus service charge and tax, coming to roughly $10,000 for 150 guests.

Ways to Save on Venue & Catering

  • Book a Friday or Sunday — save 10-25%
  • Choose a buffet or family-style instead of plated
  • Skip the cocktail hour appetizers and do passed hors d'oeuvres only
  • Consider an all-inclusive venue to avoid hidden fees
  • Limit the bar to beer and wine only

Photography & Videography

Your wedding photos and video are what you'll keep forever, so this is a category many couples choose not to skimp on.

Photography Packages

For 150 guests, you'll want at least 8 hours of coverage. Typical pricing:

  • Budget: $1,800 – $2,500 (6 hours, 1 photographer, digital files)
  • Standard: $3,000 – $4,000 (8 hours, 1 photographer, engagement shoot, album)
  • Premium: $5,000+ (10+ hours, 2 photographers, album, video teaser)

Videography Packages

Video is often an add-on or separate vendor:

  • Highlight reel (3-5 min): $1,500 – $2,500
  • Full documentary: $3,000 – $5,000
  • Same-day edit: $4,000 – $6,000

Ways to Save on Photo & Video

  • Book a newer photographer who's building their portfolio
  • Skip the album and print your own later
  • Skip videography entirely if budget is tight
  • Book both photo and video from the same company for a package discount

Attire, Beauty & Jewelry

Looking your best on your wedding day doesn't have to break the bank.

Bridal Attire

  • Wedding dress: $800 – $5,000+ (average: $1,500)
  • Alterations: $200 – $600
  • Veil & accessories: $200 – $500
  • Shoes: $100 – $250

Groom's Attire

  • Suit/tux rental: $150 – $250
  • Suit purchase: $400 – $800
  • Accessories: $100 – $200

Beauty Services

  • Bridal hair & makeup: $450 – $600 (including trials)
  • Bridesmaids hair & makeup: $150 – $200/person

Flowers & Decorations

Florals and decor set the mood of your wedding. For 150 guests, here's the typical breakdown:

  • Bridal bouquet: $150 – $400
  • Bridesmaid bouquets (5-6): $85 each
  • Centerpieces (15 tables): $60 – $200 each
  • Ceremony arch/arrangements: $300 – $1,500
  • Boutonnieres & corsages: $20 – $25 each
  • Additional decor (lighting, signage): $400 – $2,500

Total for flowers & decor (150 guests): $2,000 – $5,000

Ways to Save on Flowers

  • Choose in-season flowers
  • Use lots of greenery instead of expensive blooms
  • Repurpose ceremony flowers at the reception
  • DIY centerpieces with grocery store flowers
  • Skip the floral centerpieces and use candles or lanterns instead

Music & Entertainment

Music sets the vibe — from ceremony processional to last dance.

DJ vs. Band

  • DJ (4-5 hours): $800 – $2,200
  • Live band (4-5 hours): $2,500 – $7,000

For 150 guests, a good DJ is usually more than enough to keep the dance floor packed. Most couples spend $1,200 – $1,500 on a professional DJ with MC services.

Ceremony Music

Don't forget ceremony music — a soloist, string quartet, or guitarist for the ceremony typically costs $300 – $800.

Stationery & Invitations

For 150 guests, plan on sending about 75-80 invitations (most are for couples or families).

  • Save-the-dates: $1 – $3 each ($75 – $240)
  • Invitations (full suite): $2.50 – $15 each ($190 – $1,125)
  • Programs: $0.50 – $2.50 each ($60 – $300)
  • Thank-you cards: $1.50 – $4 each ($170 – $460)
  • Postage: ~$0.73 each ($110 total)

Total stationery budget (150 guests): $500 – $2,000

Miscellaneous & Hidden Costs

The biggest mistake couples make is forgetting about these "small" expenses that add up fast:

  • Marriage license: $30 – $150
  • Officiant fee: $200 – $600
  • Wedding rings: $500 – $3,000+
  • Wedding party gifts: $50 – $125/person
  • Parent gifts: $100 – $200 each set
  • Favors: $2 – $5/guest ($300 – $750 for 150)
  • Day-of coordinator: $1,500 – $2,500
  • Gratuities: 15-20% of catering, $20-50 per vendor

Always build in a 5-10% buffer for unexpected costs. Trust us — something will come up.

10 Ways to Save Money on Your 150-Guest Wedding

  1. Choose a non-Saturday date: Friday or Sunday weddings save 10-25% on venue costs
  2. Opt for a brunch or lunch reception: Daytime catering is 20-30% cheaper than dinner
  3. Limit the plus-ones: Only allow plus-ones for married/engaged couples
  4. DIY what you can: Centerpieces, favors, and decor can all be DIY projects
  5. Use grocery store flowers: Trader Joe's and Costco have great wedding flower packages
  6. Skip the cake: Or do a small cutting cake and sheet cake for serving
  7. Skip the favors: Most guests forget them anyway
  8. Book a newer photographer: Great work, lower prices as they build portfolio
  9. Borrow or rent decor: Facebook Marketplace and wedding resale groups are goldmines
  10. Negotiate with vendors: Ask about off-peak discounts or package deals

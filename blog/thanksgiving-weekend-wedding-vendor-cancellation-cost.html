Your caterer called on Monday. The wedding is Saturday, November 28, 88 people are flying in, and half the balance is already paid. That is the situation this page is built for.

A last-minute wedding vendor replacement cost runs 35% to 60% over contract price on the Saturday after Thanksgiving, and the reason is not greed. It is that every kitchen within 40 miles sold that date in August. A caterer contracted at $105 a head quotes $152 a head for 88 guests, or $4,136 more than you planned to spend. Most couples cannot find one at all.

So the answer splits in two. If a replacement exists, budget $4,000 to $5,500 for the caterer and treat every other vendor as a separate decision. If it does not, drop-off food plus four hired servers feeds the same 88 people for about $2,500. Both paths start with the same call, and it is not to the caterer.

What a Last-Minute Wedding Vendor Replacement Cost Looks Like

The numbers below come from a November 28, 2026 wedding with 120 invited and 88 attending, quotes gathered in the five days before the date. The original column is what the couple signed. The replacement column is the first credible quote that answered inside 48 hours.

Vendor Original contract 5-day replacement Extra Can you get one?
Caterer, plated dinner $9,240 ($105/head) $13,376 ($152/head) +$4,136 (45%) One in five answers. Hotel wants a $14,000 minimum
Photographer, 8 hours $2,850 $3,900 +$1,050 (37%) Yes, through an associate network inside 48 hours
DJ, 5 hours $1,600 $2,100 +$500 (31%) Sometimes. Every DJ in town is booked that Saturday
Florist, 14 tables $4,200 $1,900 Saves $2,300 Grocery florist, yes. Not the design you approved
Cake and dessert $689 $180 Saves $509 Supermarket sheet cakes, same day
Rentals, chairs and linens $1,340 $1,340, pickup only $0 Stock exists. Delivery does not. You need a truck
Shuttle, two coaches $2,982 Not available n/a No. Drivers were committed in September

Two lines deserve a second look. Dessert and flowers can be replaced for less than the original because the substitute is a different product, not a premium version of the same one. A supermarket cake is not a $689 cake, and nobody at the reception will count tiers.

The shuttle is the line with no answer. Charter companies book Thanksgiving weekend in September, and the driver who would cover your run is already on another wedding. Budget for rideshare codes instead, and tell guests early.

The caterer is where the money goes. A hotel banquet department will take the date because it has staff on property, but it prices the way hotels price: $138 a head all in, per our Thanksgiving wedding menu cost breakdown, plus a food and beverage minimum near $14,000. On 88 guests that minimum is the invoice, whether you eat it or not.

Your Emergency Fund Covers One Vendor, Not Two

Most wedding checklists tell you to hold 5% to 10% of the budget for surprises. On a $34,200 wedding, the national average in The Knot's 2026 Real Weddings Study, that is $1,710 to $3,420.

Run the emergency scenarios against that fund and the rule of thumb falls apart.

What breaks Extra cost Against a $3,420 fund Against a $5,130 fund
Photographer cancels, associate plus editing $1,050 Covered, 31% of fund Covered, 20%
DJ cancels $500 Covered, 15% Covered, 10%
Venue adds holiday staffing surcharge $1,230 Covered, 36% Covered, 24%
Caterer cancels, replacement found $4,136 $716 short Covered, 81%
Caterer cancels and no replacement exists $2,500 fallback Covered, 73% Covered, 49%
Caterer plus photographer $5,186 $1,766 short $56 short

So the honest number for a holiday-week date is 15%, not 10%. That is $5,130 on the same wedding, and it survives two failures instead of one. On a June Saturday, 10% is fine. Peak-week pricing changes the arithmetic, not the principle.

Where the fund should sit matters too. Cash in a savings account you can reach on a Sunday beats a brokerage transfer that takes three business days. The wedding savings calculator will tell you what to set aside monthly, and the budget calculator shows the damage when a single line moves by 45%.

The Clause That Decides Whether You Pay Anything

Before you call a replacement, read two paragraphs of the contract. They are usually on page two, in the section titled Cancellation or Substitution.

The substitution clause. Many caterers and photographers reserve the right to send a comparable substitute, with your deposit transferring to that person. If yours has that language, you may not need a new vendor at all. Ask in writing who the substitute is, what they charge, and whether the deposit follows. A caterer with three crews can staff your date from the second crew and never tell you until Thursday.

Force majeure. This covers weather, illness, and government orders. It does not cover a bigger job that came in, and it does not cover a chef who quit. If the cancellation letter cites force majeure and the reason is staffing, that letter is wrong, and saying so politely usually produces a better offer than a lawyer would.

Insurance is the third lever, and it is out of reach. Most special-event cancellation policies must be bought 14 to 30 days before the event, and several carriers require 21. At five days nobody underwrites a risk that has already happened. Liability coverage through the venue still works, and it pays for injuries and property, not for a missing caterer. Our wedding insurance calculator shows what the two coverages cost when you buy them at the right time, which is 60 days out, not now.

Payment method decides your last resort. A card charge gives you a dispute right under federal rules within 60 days of the statement showing the charge, and many issuers allow 120. A Zelle transfer, a cash envelope, or a check gives you nothing but a phone call. Deposits paid by card are the only ones with a lever behind them.

Thanksgiving Week Is the Worst Week to Replace Anything

The holiday does not just raise prices. It closes the supply chain, and each vendor type closes on a different day.

Vendor type Last realistic booking What closes the door
Rental company Friday, November 20 Trucks load Wednesday, warehouse shut Thursday. After that, pickup only
Custom bakery Tuesday, November 17 Ovens run pies all week. Ten-day lead time on a decorated cake
Caterer, new event Monday, November 23 Turkeys ordered, crews assigned. Staffing agency cutoff is Tuesday
Staffing agency, servers Tuesday, November 24 Holiday crews go out Monday. Four-hour minimums, 1.5x rate
Wholesale florist Wednesday, November 25 by noon Market closes early, no Thursday delivery. Friday is leftovers
Photographer Friday, November 27 Associate shooters take work with 12 hours' notice

The staffing line is the one people underestimate. A replacement caterer who has no crew will sub-contract servers at holiday rates: $26 to $32 an hour, billed at 1.5x, with a four-hour minimum. Six servers and two bartenders for six hours comes to $2,160 in labor before a single plate is washed. Some quotes bury that inside the per-head price. Some list it as a separate line on the final invoice.

Supermarkets are the exception that saves the day, with a catch. Thanksgiving week is the deli's biggest week of the year, and heat-and-serve orders close the Sunday before. Whole cooked turkeys are gone by Tuesday. If you are reading this on Wednesday, the answer is rotisserie chickens and trays of sides, not a turkey.

The Fallback If It Is Already Too Late

Wednesday night, no caterer, 88 guests. This plan costs $2,500 to $3,200 and it works.

Food. Restaurant drop-off catering is the best version of this. Family-style pans from a local restaurant run $18 to $26 a person, delivered, no service. For 88 that is $1,584 to $2,288. Warehouse club trays land at $12 to $18 a person, which is $1,056 to $1,584, and they require somebody to pick them up and keep them hot.

People. Four servers from a staffing agency for five hours at holiday rates is roughly $900. They plate drop-off food, clear tables, and keep the buffet from turning into a scrum. This is the line that decides whether the night feels catered or feels like a potluck.

Photographs. Two friends with real cameras plus a stack of instant film beats an empty album. Book a portrait session in your wedding clothes two weeks later for $250 to $400 and you have the formal frames. Our Thanksgiving weekend photography cost guide covers what an associate shooter charges when you have 48 hours instead of 12 months.

Music. A rented PA for $250 to $400 and a playlist built by the one friend who cares about it. Nobody remembers the DJ. They remember whether the room had sound.

What you do not do is move the date. Rescheduling inside a holiday week costs more than the fallback, because every guest has already paid for travel, and the venue's next open Saturday is in April. Move the food, not the wedding.

Last-Minute Wedding Vendor Replacement Cost in a $34,200 Budget

Against the $34,200 national average, a $4,136 caterer replacement is 12.1% of the entire wedding, spent on food you had already budgeted once. Add the photographer and you are at $5,186, or 15.2%. That is the gap between a wedding that absorbs a bad week and one that goes into debt for it.

Two moves make the week survivable. First, hold 15% instead of 10% if your date falls between Thanksgiving and New Year's, and keep it in cash. Second, get the substitution clause in writing at signing, while the vendor still wants your review. A caterer who will commit to naming a substitute in the contract has three crews. The one who will not has one.

Then check the rest of the plan for single points of failure. The vendor comparison tool puts two quotes side by side, which is how you spot a caterer quoting $105 today and $152 in an emergency.

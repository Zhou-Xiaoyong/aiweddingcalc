Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how AI Wedding Calc ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit aiweddingcalc.com (the "Site").

1. Information We Collect

Calculator inputs. All calculator computations run entirely in your browser (client-side). The numbers you enter — budgets, guest counts, dates — are never transmitted to or stored on our servers.

Usage data. Like most websites, we automatically collect certain anonymous information through analytics tools, including pages visited, approximate geographic region, device and browser type, and referring pages.

Information you provide. If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to share (such as your email address and message content).

2. Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the Site. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to collect anonymous usage statistics. You can learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and opt out via the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

3. Advertising

We may display advertising on the Site, including ads served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this Site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

4. Affiliate Links

AI Wedding Calc is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com. As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases. When you click an affiliate link, the retailer may set cookies to attribute your purchase; this does not change the price you pay.

5. Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. We use cookies for analytics and (where applicable) advertising as described above. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies will not affect the functionality of our calculators.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. Anonymous usage data is processed by the third-party services listed above under their respective privacy policies.

7. Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location (e.g., under GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you. Because we store essentially no personal data, most requests can be resolved by clearing your browser cookies. For anything else, contact us.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our contact page.

Frequently Asked Questions

A sparkler send-off costs $100-300 for 100 guests. You need 36-inch sparklers ($15-25 per box of 8), a bucket of sand, and signage.
Bubbles and ribbon wands are the most affordable at $30-80 for 100 guests. Flower petals and lavender runs $50-150, while sparklers cost $100-300.
36-inch sparklers are safest for weddings as they have longer handles and burn times (2-3 minutes). Always have a bucket of water or sand nearby and check venue policies first.

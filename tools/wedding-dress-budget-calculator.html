💎 Bridal Accessories

Frequently Asked Questions

The average US bride spends $1,600-$2,000 on her wedding dress, with prices ranging from $500 to $10,000+.
Alterations typically cost $200-$800 depending on the complexity. Budget at least $500 for a structured gown.
Bridal accessories typically cost $300-800 total. A veil runs $100-400, shoes $80-250, jewelry $50-200, and a sash or belt $50-150.
A veil is optional but remains the most popular bridal accessory. Cathedral veils create drama, while birdcage veils suit vintage styles. Many brides opt for hair pieces instead.
Designer shoes ($200+) are beautiful but rarely visible under a long gown. Consider comfortable mid-range shoes ($80-150) and invest the savings elsewhere.

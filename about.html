About AI Wedding Calc

Helping couples plan with confidence — one number at a time

Our Mission

Planning a wedding involves hundreds of decisions, and almost every one of them has a price tag attached. AI Wedding Calc exists to answer one simple question for engaged couples: "How much will this actually cost?"

Unlike all-in-one wedding platforms, we focus on doing one thing exceptionally well — building the most accurate, detailed wedding calculators on the internet. Every tool is free, requires no sign-up, and runs entirely in your browser.

What We Offer

  • 50+ specialized calculators covering budget, guest list, catering, alcohol, venue, flowers, photography, stationery, entertainment, and more.
  • Real US wedding data. Our estimates are benchmarked against industry research such as The Knot Real Weddings Study and regional cost surveys, and reviewed periodically to stay current.
  • AI-powered suggestions. Each result comes with practical, personalized tips to help you save money without sacrificing your vision.
  • Planning guides. Our blog breaks down wedding costs by state, catering strategies, timelines, and DIY ideas.

How Our Calculators Work

Every calculator uses transparent formulas built from published industry averages, adjusted for factors like region, season, day of week, guest count, and formality level. Results are estimates intended for planning purposes — your actual quotes will vary by vendor and market.

Editorial & Data Standards

We review our cost data against multiple public sources and update our models when major industry studies are released. If you spot a number that looks off, let us know — we take accuracy seriously.
